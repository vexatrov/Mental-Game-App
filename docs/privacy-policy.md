# Trader's Mind: Privacy Policy

_Effective September 25, 2026_

Trader's Mind is a private notebook for your trading psychology. It keeps what you write on your phone. The developer never sees it.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no account or sign-in. It has no servers, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The developer receives no data about you or your use of the app.

## Where your entries are stored

- **On your device.** Journal entries, Mental Hand Histories, emotion maps, sessions, routines, drill cards and settings are kept in the app's private storage. Other apps can't read them.
- **Daily snapshots.** The app keeps copies of your data from the last 7 days in its private storage, so you can roll back a mistake.
- **Backups you choose.** You can export a backup file and share it with any app you pick. You can also turn on automatic backup and pick a file for it, for example in Google Drive or on your phone. The app writes that file and nothing else. Once a file is in another service, that service's privacy policy applies.
- **Android device backup.** If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is managed by Google. You control this in your phone's settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** used for the check-in reminders you schedule during a trading session. They are created and shown on your phone.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact timing):** optional. Lets reminders fire on time instead of roughly on time.
- **Run at startup:** re-creates your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.

The app doesn't use the internet. Links such as the book page, this policy and the source code open in your browser.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Trader's Mind → Storage. Any backup files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. The app will not start collecting data without an update to this policy and to its Google Play listing.
