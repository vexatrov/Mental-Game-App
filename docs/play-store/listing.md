## Full description (max 4000)

```
Trader's Mind is a private notebook for the mental side of trading. Emotions like fear, greed, tilt, overconfidence and lost discipline are signals. This app helps you read them, trace them back to their cause and correct them, one trading day at a time.

It is built on the system Jared Tendler lays out in his book The Mental Game of Trading. The tools are his; this app gives you a place to use them every day. It works on its own, but it makes the most sense alongside the book.

BEFORE THE OPEN
• A warm-up checklist: read your emotion maps, rehearse a recent trigger, rate the emotion you carried over and start your check-in timer
• Correction drill: flashcards made from your own Mental Hand Histories, repeated until the corrections come without effort

WHILE YOU TRADE
• Quick notes tagged by problem: greed, fear, tilt, confidence or discipline
• A session timer with check-in reminders, so emotion can't build unnoticed
• Reset: a four-step flow for the moment you feel it building. Spot the level on your map, break the momentum, read your correction, then go back to trading or stop for the day.

AFTER THE CLOSE
• A cool-down: score the quality of your decisions from 1 to 100, not your P&L
• Tag mistakes as A-, B- or C-game
• Expand quick notes into a full pattern: trigger, thoughts, emotions, behaviour, and how your decisions and view of the market changed

GO DEEPER
• Emotion maps: describe each problem across levels 1 to 10, with the mental side and the technical side next to each other. Revise them as you learn, and keep the history.
• Mental Hand History: a five-step worksheet that takes one problem down to the flawed logic underneath it and writes the correction
• A- to C-game analysis: define your best, typical and worst game, then measure each session against it
• Inchworm chart: watch your range of decision quality move forward month by month

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your phone.
• Automatic backup to a file you choose, for example in Google Drive
• Daily snapshots for the last 7 days, and export or import any time
• Light and dark themes

IMPORTANT
Trader's Mind is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Jared Tendler or his publisher. It is a self-help journal, not therapy or medical care, and nothing in it is financial or investment advice. Trading involves risk of loss.
```
