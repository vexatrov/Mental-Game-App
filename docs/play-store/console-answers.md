# Play Console answers

Answers for **Policy → App content** and **Store settings**, based on what the app actually does. Answer honestly if the form asks something not listed here.

## Privacy policy
`https://github.com/vexatrov/Mental-Game-App/blob/HEAD/docs/privacy-policy.md`

## App access
**All functionality is available without any access restrictions.** There is no login.

## Ads
**No, my app does not contain ads.**

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
- Category: **All other app types**
- Violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, gambling: **No** to all
- Users can interact or exchange content with each other: **No** (entries never leave the phone)
- Shares the user's location: **No**
- Lets users buy digital goods: **No** (change this when Pro launches)

Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

## Target audience and content
- Age groups: **18 and over** only. This keeps the app out of the Families program, and trading is an adult activity.
- Could the store listing unintentionally appeal to children: **No**

## Data safety
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**

Play then shows "No data collected" and "No data shared". This is accurate:
- Everything is stored and processed only on the phone. Play doesn't count data that never leaves the device.
- Backups go only where the user sends them, through Android's own file picker and share sheet. The developer never receives them.
- There are no analytics, crash-reporting or ad SDKs. The release build doesn't even have the INTERNET permission.

## Advertising ID
**No**, the app doesn't use an advertising ID.

## Financial features
**My app doesn't provide any of these financial features.** It is a journal: no trading, brokerage, payments or advice.

## Health apps
It is a trading journal, not a medical app. If the form asks for wellness categories, **Stress management, relaxation and mental acuity** is the honest fit for the Reset flow. It is general wellness, and the app already says it isn't therapy or medical care. Leave every medical category unticked.

## News app, government app
**No** to both.

## Permissions the review may ask about
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Check-in reminders during a session the user starts |
| Schedule exact alarms | Optional; reminders arrive on time. The app works without it. |
| Run at startup | Re-schedules pending reminders after a reboot |

No declaration is needed for any of these. (The restricted `USE_EXACT_ALARM` is **not** used.)

## Store settings
- App or game: **App**. Category: **Productivity**
- Free or paid: **Free**. A free app can't later become paid, but it can add in-app purchases, which is the Pro-later plan.
- Countries: all
