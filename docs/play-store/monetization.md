# Making money from Trader's Mind

The plan is to **launch free and add Pro later**. The ground rule: Jared Tendler's tools stay free. Pro charges for convenience and extra depth, never for access to his method.

## Options, best fit first

### 1. Partner with Jared Tendler (biggest upside)
An official or endorsed companion app is worth far more than anything done alone. He has the audience, and the app gives his readers somewhere to practise. Possible shapes:
- A mention on his site or newsletter. Downloads come from his audience.
- Revenue share on Pro.
- An affiliate or referral arrangement for his book, courses or coaching.
- He licenses or co-brands it.

Ask **before** charging anything. See the email draft below.

### 2. Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing
Google keeps about **15%**. Ideas that don't lock away his tools:
- Insights: which triggers, times of day or problems show up most, and how scores trend
- Reports: a PDF or CSV of a week or month to review, or to share with a coach
- A home-screen widget: one tap to Reset or to start the timer
- Extra reminder types, custom map templates and themes
- Sync across devices (this needs a server, so it would be a subscription)

Pricing that is typical for this niche: **$6.99–9.99 one-time**, or **$2.99/month or $19.99/year** if sync is included. One-time purchases suit a private offline journal better.

Realistic numbers: niche journal apps usually convert 2–5% of active users. For example, 5,000 installs × 3% × $7.99, after Google's cut, comes to about **$1,000**. Downloads are what move this number, which is why reaching Tendler's audience matters.

### 3. Small extras
- **Book affiliate link** on the About screen, for example Amazon Associates. It earns about $1 per copy and needs a disclosure line. It is tiny, but it fits "read the book first".
- **Tip jar**: an optional "Support the developer" in-app purchase.

### Not recommended
- **Ads.** They break the "no ads, no tracking" promise the privacy policy and listing make, would change the Data safety answers, and earn very little in a small niche.
- **Selling or analysing user data.** Never. The app's value is that it's private.

## Before adding Pro
- [ ] Get Tendler's answer, or at least give him the chance to object.
- [ ] Set up a **payments profile** in Play Console (tax and bank details). Google may then show your address publicly on the listing.
- [ ] The source code is public. Before paid features go in, **make the repo private** and move the privacy policy to a public page (a small public repo or GitHub Pages). Also remove the "Source code" link on the About screen.
- [ ] Update the content rating ("digital purchases: yes") and the listing.
- [ ] Keep every existing free feature free. Taking features away from early users loses their trust.
